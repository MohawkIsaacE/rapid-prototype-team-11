# **Gravity Dungeon**
# Team 11
## Desired Game Mechanic
The desired game mechanic for this project is the ability to flip the player character's gravity at will to avoid obstacles instead of a traditional jump. The player will have complete control over side-to-side movement, with vertical movement revolving around this mechanic. Once the player switches the gravity, they will be locked out of switching again until they land on the floor or ceiling, refreshing the ability.

## Objective Statement
Our objective statement for this design is to answer the question; Does the mechanic allow for challenging gameplay that the player can master, or is it too simple to serve as a challenge?

## Intended Experience
The kind of experience we have in mind is an easy to learn, difficult to master type of gameplay. We find this mechanic to be unique as it requires players to think differently about how they're going to get past obstacles with one flip of gravity. The player will have to be quick and precise to succeed.

## Design Rationale
For the bigger game that this mechanic would be attached to, we were thinking of a Metroidvania type game, where the gravity switch would be a new ability gained by the player to traverse the world better and unlock new, previously inaccessible areas.
