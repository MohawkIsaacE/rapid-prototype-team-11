
**Desired Game Mechanic**
The desired game mechanic for this project is the ability to change a character's gravity at will to avoid obstacles instead of a traditional jump. The player will have complete control over side-to-side movement, but vertical movement will revolve around this mechanic. After the player does a gravity switch, there'll be a cooldown of a second or so (we'll find the exact time with some testing) before they can switch again.

**Objective Statement**
Our objective statement for this design is to answer the question; is this mechanic difficult enough to provide the player a challenge to master? Or is it too easy/simple?

**Intended Experience**
The kind of experience I have in mind is an easy to learn, difficult to master type of gameplay. This mechanic is unique because it requires players to think differently about how they're going to get past the obstacles with that cooldown in mind. They'll have to be creative and resourceful to overcome these difficult challenges.

**Design Rationale**
For the bigger game that this mechanic would be attached to, I was thinking of a Metroid Vania type game, where the gravity switch would be a new ability gained by the player to traverse the world better and unlock new, previously inaccessible areas.
